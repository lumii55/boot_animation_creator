Tips for Beginners

  • Requirement: To apply the animation, your phone must be Rooted (Magisk, KernelSU, etc). If you don't know what this is, research before trying!
  • Samsung Devices: Samsung uses proprietary .qmg formats. The file generated here might require a Custom ROM or Custom Kernel to work on Samsung.
  • Size matters: Avoid files larger than 20MB. Heavy files can cause Android to stutter or even cause bootloops.
  • Screen aspect: Phones are vertical! Prefer vertical videos (TikTok/Reels format) so your animation doesn't get stretched.
  • Performance: Keeping resolution at 'Light' and 24-30 FPS ensures a fast and smooth boot.
